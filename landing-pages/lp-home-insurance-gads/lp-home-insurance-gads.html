02.

Additional Coverage

Emergency Mortgage Assistance

If your home is uninhabitable after a covered loss, the policy can provide three months of mortgage payments, up to $10,000.

Utility Line

Protects you from the costs if the utility lines on your property (like water, gas, electric, or sewer lines) get damaged. This coverage helps pay for repairs, excavation, and even restoring your yard or driveway after the work is done.

Equipment Breakdown

This covers the perils of mechanical, electrical and pressure systems breakdown, which are typically excluded or limited perils in the homeowner's policy.

Extended Replacement Cost

Your policy has a maximum cost for rebuilding after a covered loss, but costs may rise due to inflation or disasters. With Extended Replacement Cost, we'll cover up to 25% above your policy limit if rebuilding costs exceed it.
